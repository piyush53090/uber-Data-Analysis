 🚖 Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Uber booking data and uncover meaningful business insights. It enables stakeholders to monitor booking performance, revenue, trip efficiency, customer behavior, cancellation patterns, and vehicle utilization through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Ride-hailing companies process thousands of bookings daily. Without an interactive reporting system, identifying revenue trends, operational bottlenecks, customer preferences, and cancellation patterns becomes difficult.

This dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### Executive Overview
Monitor overall business performance using KPIs such as:

- Total Bookings
- Total Revenue
- Average Booking Value
- Total Trip Distance
- Average Trip Distance
- Average Trip Time

---

### Booking Analysis

Analyze booking performance to understand:

- Total completed and cancelled rides
- Booking trends over time
- Daily ride demand
- Overall booking distribution

---

### Revenue Analysis

Identify revenue-driving segments by analyzing:

- Revenue generated by each vehicle category
- Average booking value
- Revenue trends across different time periods

---

### Vehicle Performance

Compare different vehicle categories including:

- Auto
- Bike
- Sedan
- SUV
- Premier
- XL

The dashboard helps identify:

- Most booked vehicle type
- Highest revenue generating category
- Average trip distance by vehicle
- Average trip duration by vehicle

---

### Cancellation Analysis

Understand operational challenges by analyzing:

Customer cancellations

- Driver taking too long
- Change of plans
- Wrong pickup location
- Other customer reasons

Driver cancellations

- Customer not reachable
- Vehicle issues
- Traffic conditions
- Other operational reasons

This helps identify the major causes behind lost bookings.

---

### Customer Insights

Analyze customer behavior through:

- Payment method preferences
- Vehicle preferences
- Customer ratings
- Booking trends

---

## 📈 Key Insights Generated

This dashboard helps answer business questions such as:

- Which vehicle category generates the highest revenue?
- Which vehicle receives the maximum bookings?
- How do bookings change over time?
- What percentage of rides are successfully completed?
- What are the primary reasons behind ride cancellations?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- Which vehicle type has the longest average trip distance?
- Which category contributes the highest booking value?
- How do trip duration and distance vary across vehicle types?
